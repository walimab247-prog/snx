What the market desk covers

The market desk exists to answer one question every morning: what changed, and does it matter? We track spot and perpetual volumes across the top venues, monitor stablecoin supply as a proxy for available dry powder, and watch the spread between centralised and on-chain pricing for signs of stress.

Rather than reporting headline prices, we focus on structure. A five percent move on thin weekend liquidity means something very different from the same move against a deep book during the London session, and our coverage says which one you are looking at.

Tokenized real-world assets have become a permanent part of the beat. Supply on consumer-facing chains has grown quickly enough that it now influences liquidity conditions in adjacent markets, and we report the raw numbers alongside the caveats about how they are counted.

  • Daily liquidity and volatility briefing across major venues.
  • Stablecoin supply, redemption flows and peg health monitoring.
  • Tokenized equity and treasury products tracked by issuer.
  • Derivatives funding, open interest and liquidation clusters.

How we source market data

Pricing is aggregated from several independent providers and reconciled before anything is published. When feeds disagree by more than a small tolerance we publish the range and explain the discrepancy instead of quietly choosing one number.

On-chain metrics — supply, transfers, bridge flows — are computed from indexed node data rather than lifted from dashboards, which lets us show the exact query behind a claim when a reader asks for it.

Nothing on this desk is a recommendation. We describe conditions and mechanisms; portfolio decisions are yours.
