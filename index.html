Common Queries

Need Help?

Get answers regarding our reporting methodology and subscription services.

How do you verify hack reports?

Every incident is confirmed against on-chain evidence before publication: the exploit transaction, the affected contract and the destination addresses. Reports that rest only on social media claims stay unpublished until the chain data supports them.

Can I subscribe to alerts?

Yes. Security alerts reach email subscribers and our social channels simultaneously, usually within minutes of verification and well before the full post-mortem is published. The daily briefing is free.

Do you offer an institutional API?

Our reconciled market data and the full security incident dataset are available under a commercial licence, delivered through the same API that powers our newsroom. Licensing never affects editorial decisions.

Do you publish sponsored articles?

No. We do not accept payment for coverage and we do not run contributed posts. Commercial partnerships are clearly labelled and kept entirely off the news pages.

How do I report a correction?

Send the article link and the disputed claim to our newsroom address. Verified corrections are applied in place with a dated note explaining exactly what changed.
